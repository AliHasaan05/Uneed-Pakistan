Join the Movement

Be the Reason Someone Smiles

Our volunteers are students, teachers, doctors, shopkeepers and professionals from Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Quetta, Multan and beyond. They pack rations, teach after school, register patients at camps and help families through difficult days. You do not need special experience — just a few hours and a willingness to help your neighbours.

Volunteers

Give Your Time and Skills in Your Own City

Whether you have two hours on a weekend or can lead an emergency relief team during floods, your presence makes an immediate tangible difference for Pakistani families in need.

Our volunteer community represents the heart and soul of Umeed Pakistan Foundation. We organize regular food packaging drives, medical health camp registrations, school tutoring classes, and tree plantation drives across all provinces.
