About the foundation

Creating Hope Across Pakistan

Umeed Pakistan Foundation works alongside local communities — from the villages of Punjab and Sindh to the mountains of Gilgit-Baltistan — to make everyday essentials reachable for families who need them most.

Our Mission

To empower underserved communities across Pakistan by providing meaningful access to education, healthcare, food, clean water and essential support.

Our Vision

A Pakistan where every individual has the opportunity to live with dignity, hope and opportunity.

Our Vision

A Pakistan Where Every Individual Lives With Dignity

We envision thriving communities where poverty does not dictate a child's future, and where access to food, clean water, and medical care is guaranteed for all.

📚

Universal Education

Breaking the cycle of illiteracy through community-led village schools, school bags, learning supplies, and girl-child scholarships.

🩺

Accessible Healthcare

Organizing free medical diagnosis camps, maternal healthcare checkups, and free medicines in remote and underserved areas.

💧

Clean Water & Food

Installing hand pumps in drought-stricken areas and distributing monthly ration packages so families never go hungry.

Strategic Goals

Our Core Objectives

Specific, targeted commitments through which we translate our vision into real daily results across Pakistan.

Objective 1

Quality Education For Underserved Children

Equipping rural schools with furniture, clean drinking water, books, and uniforms to prevent student dropouts in rural Punjab and KPK.

Objective 2

Primary Healthcare & Medical Camps

Bringing doctors, diagnostic testing, maternal health support, and essential medicines directly to mountain and desert villages.

Objective 3

Food Security & Monthly Ration Assistance

Ensuring vulnerable households receive nutritious staples (flour, rice, oil, pulses) through dignified community distribution.

Objective 4

Emergency Relief & Disaster Resilience

Mobilizing immediate emergency supplies, shelter, and dry food within hours of floods, earthquakes, or severe winter cold waves.

Objective 5

Orphans & Vulnerable Children (Kafalat)

Providing monthly education, nutritional, and emotional support stipends to orphans and vulnerable children across all provinces.

Objective 6

15-City Youth Volunteer Network

Mobilizing university students and local professionals into trained community leaders who coordinate transparent on-ground relief.
